Product Customization Services

Tailored solutions to bring your unique product vision to life.

FiadShop offers comprehensive product customization solutions designed to meet distinct branding and product development requirements. The available services are categorized into three primary tiers, each catering to different business scales and creative needs.

1. Custom Packaging

This service focuses exclusively on branding the exterior packaging to enhance unboxing experiences.

  • Customization Scope: Branded boxes, poly bags, custom labels, stickers, inserts, and user manuals based on provided design assets.
  • Process: After logging into the FiadShop dashboard, packaging designs are uploaded via the dedicated customization section. The selected packaging is then linked to the relevant product profiles within the inventory system.
  • Cost Note: Printing costs are generally included within the total packaging price.

2. Print on Demand (POD)

This service enables printing on individual items without requiring a minimum order quantity (No MOQ). A single unit can be fully customized.

  • Customization Scope: Direct printing of logos, images, or unique designs onto products such as T-shirts, mugs, phone cases, and tote bags.
  • Process: Available products are selected from the Print-on-Demand module. The built-in design tool allows for direct artwork creation or upload, after which the finished variant is ready for listing in the store.

3. Original Design Manufacturing (ODM)

This is the most advanced tier, allowing for deep modifications to the product itself, including appearance, materials, dimensions, colors, and internal features. ODM is offered in two levels:

  • Basic ODM: Ideal for testing new concepts. Works with a standard supplier network to execute general customizations.
  • Premium Advanced ODM: Designed for established brand builders. This includes a dedicated support team, comparative analysis between 3–5 vetted manufacturers, and full control over both product and packaging specifications.

ODM Workflow

  1. Request Submission: A customization request is submitted through the platform, specifying product details, target pricing, and required modifications.
  2. Sample Inspection: Upon approval, a physical or video sample is produced and sent for verification.
  3. SKU Generation: After the sample is approved, a unique SKU is created exclusively for that merchant's listings.

Important Terms and Conditions

  • Copyright Liability: The merchant bears full legal responsibility for ensuring that all uploaded designs, logos, and concepts do not infringe upon any third-party intellectual property or copyrights.
  • Exclusivity Clause: For customized products, if no orders are placed within three (3) months of production setup, FiadShop reserves the right to make the product design publicly available to other users.
  • Non-Refundable Fees: Sample production fees, mold creation fees, or setup charges are non-refundable, even if the final sample does not meet the merchant's expectations.

For detailed guidance on specific categories (such as electronics or fashion) or to initiate a customization project, assistance is available through the FiadShop Help Center or via live chat support.
